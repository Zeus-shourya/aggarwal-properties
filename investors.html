Questions

Things investors ask us.

How do I know which plots are still available?

Open the Projects page. Available plots are green and sold plots are red. Tap any plot to see its details. Final availability is always confirmed on WhatsApp before booking.

Can I buy more than one plot?

Yes. Tell us how many plots or how much area you want and we will check which adjoining plots are available.

Where are the prices?

Rates depend on the project, the plot size and its position, such as a corner or a wider road. We share current prices directly on WhatsApp.

Can I visit the site before booking?

Yes. Message us and we will arrange a visit so you can see the plot and its surroundings yourself.

How do I book a plot?

Send the plot number on WhatsApp to +91 70150 70104. We will confirm that it is available, share the price and guide you through the booking.

Do you deal in larger land parcels?

Yes. Beyond these plotted projects, Aggarwal Properties has transacted over 150 acres of land. Share your requirement on WhatsApp.
