About us

Ten years in land, one plot at a time.

Aggarwal Properties deals in plots and land: residential colonies, industrial plots and larger parcels. In more than a decade of work, over 150 acres have been transacted.

Our story

Built on deals done, not promises made.

Aggarwal Properties was started by Aman Goel with a simple way of working: know the land, know the paperwork, and tell the buyer exactly what they are getting.

That approach has carried the firm through more than ten years and over 150 acres of land transactions, from single residential plots to industrial plots and larger holdings.

Today every project we offer is published plot by plot. You can open the layout, tap a plot and read its area, dimensions and facing for yourself, and see at a glance whether it is still available.
