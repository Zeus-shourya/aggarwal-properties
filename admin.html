Website editor

Mark plots as sold, available or available for resale yourself. No coding. The website updates on its own a minute or two after you save.

Open the editor → Try it on the map first

Mark a plot as sold or for resale

Green = available Yellow = available for resale Red = sold

  1. Open the editor with the button above and log in with the email that was invited. ऊपर वाले बटन से Editor खोलें और invite वाले email से login करें।
  2. Open aggarwal-properties, then click Sold & resale plots on the left. बाईं तरफ़ Sold & resale plots पर क्लिक करें।
  3. Each project (MANSA - 06, MANSA - 05, MANSA - 04) has two boxes: SOLD plots (red) and plots available for RESALE (yellow). Click the right box, type the plot number and pick it from the list. Repeat for every plot. हर project के दो box हैं: SOLD (लाल) और RESALE (पीला)। सही box में plot number लिखें और list में से चुनें।
  4. Click Save. In about 1–2 minutes the website shows the sold plots in red and the resale plots in yellow. Every plot that is in neither box stays green. Save दबाएँ। 1–2 मिनट में sold plot लाल और resale plot पीले दिखेंगे। बाकी सब हरे (available) रहेंगे।

Add a property to "Properties with us"

  1. In the editor, click Properties with us on the left, then Add an entry. Editor में बाईं तरफ़ Properties with us खोलें और Add an entry दबाएँ।
  2. Fill in the title, type, sale or rent, location, size and price. Write size and price exactly as you want them shown. Leave price empty to show "On request". Title, type, sale/rent, location, size और price भरें। Price खाली छोड़ेंगे तो "On request" दिखेगा।
  3. Under Photos, upload pictures from your phone or computer. The first photo is the cover. Photos में फ़ोटो upload करें। पहली फ़ोटो cover बनेगी।
  4. Click Save. In about 1–2 minutes it appears under Projects → Properties with us on the website. Save दबाएँ। 1–2 मिनट में वेबसाइट पर Projects → Properties with us में दिखेगा।

Good to know

Plot available again?

Click the small × next to that plot number to remove it from its box, then Save. A plot in neither box is green again.

Sold plot now up for resale?

Add it to the RESALE box. You can leave it in the SOLD box too: a plot in both shows yellow.

Not sure which plot?

Open the map with Try it on the map first. Each click there steps a plot through green, red and yellow as a preview; nothing is saved until you use the editor.

Two plots with one number

MANSA - 05 has two plots numbered 97 and MANSA - 04 has two each of C-5, C-6 and C-7. The list shows the size beside each one so you can pick the right plot.

Property sold or off the market?

Open it under Properties with us and change Status to Sold (stays on the site with a red badge) or Hidden (removed from the site, kept in the editor).

Still showing the old colour?

Wait two minutes and refresh the page. On a phone, pull down to refresh.
